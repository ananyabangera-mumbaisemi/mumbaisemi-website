Internship 6 / 12 Months · In-person, Mumbai

Digital ASIC Intern

MumbaiSemi, SINE — IIT Bombay Campus, Powai, Mumbai

MumbaiSemi, a next-generation semiconductor chip design start-up spun out of the aiCAS Lab, IIT Bombay, is hiring motivated interns to join our team. This is a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience with cutting-edge chip design technologies, with the potential to transition into a full-time role based on performance.

Program Details

  • Duration: 6 months & 12 months options available.
  • Stipend: Monthly competitive stipend provided.
  • Location: Powai, Mumbai (Full-time, in-person at SINE, IIT Bombay).
  • Future Opportunity: Potential full-time offer based on internship performance.

Essential Skills

  • Motivated, punctual, responsible, and a team player.
  • Strong communication and writing skills.

Technical Skills

  • Digital logic design fundamentals.
  • RTL development (Verilog / SystemVerilog).
  • MATLAB / Python scripting.
  • FPGA and micro-controller/microprocessor board development.
  • Board-level digital circuit design (memory, I2C/SPI devices, ADC/DAC, etc.).
  • Debugging and problem-solving skills.
  • Verification fundamentals.

Eligibility

  • M.Tech/M.E in Electronics or related disciplines.

How to Apply

  • Submit your details via the application form below and upload your latest resume.
  • Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis until positions are filled.
  • Shortlisted candidates will be notified via email.
