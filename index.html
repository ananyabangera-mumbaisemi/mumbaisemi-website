Company Profile

About Us

MumbaiSemi is a fab-less semiconductor company designing high-performance RF, Analog, Mixed-signal, and Digital ICs for advanced communications, AI/ML hardware, autonomous vehicles, low-power IoT, and more.

Our core strength lies in developing innovative circuits to address the toughest challenges in chip design, from RF front-ends operating at extreme temperatures to high-speed PHYs built for next-generation data centers.

We research and innovate to produce disruptive solutions for futuristic challenges, developing high-quality semiconductor chips in India for both national and global needs.

Our mission

To build world-class, indigenous semiconductor IP from India, designing chips that power navigation, connectivity, and intelligent systems for a global market, while strengthening India's chip design ecosystem.

Core Hardware Focus

Applications & Products

Our chips are designed for where it matters most: satellites, vehicles, and data centers shaping the future.

Navigation

Multi-constellation GNSS front-ends supporting NavIC, GPS, Galileo and BeiDou.

Wireless

Advanced transceivers for 5G, Wi-Fi 7, and Bluetooth 5.x next-gen standards.

High-speed Comms

PHYs enabling chip-to-chip communication for PCIe and UCIe, cutting latency in AI/ML data centers.

Automotive

High-performance chips for ADAS cameras, sensor systems, and autonomous driving in extreme environments.

Flagship Architecture

DhruvaPro

The first indigenous, fully-integrated, reconfigurable RFIC for navigation, supporting NavIC, GPS, Galileo, BeiDou, and GLONASS as a single universal solution.

Chip-verified and built to production-level standards, with ESD protection, on-chip testing, and operation across extreme temperatures.

  • Multi-constellation GNSS: NavIC, GPS, Galileo, BeiDou, GLONASS
  • Chip-verified, production-level IC
  • On-chip ESD protection & built-in self-test
  • QFN-32 package for real-world evaluation
  • Rated for operation at extreme temperatures
