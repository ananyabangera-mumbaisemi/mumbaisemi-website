About us

What we do

MumbaiSemi is a fab-less semiconductor company designing high-performance RF, Analog, Mixed-signal, and Digital ICs for advanced communications, AI/ML hardware, autonomous vehicles, low-power IoT, and more.

Our core strength lies in developing innovative circuits to address the toughest challenges in chip design — from RF front-ends operating at extreme temperatures to high-speed PHYs built for next-generation data centers.

We research and innovate to produce disruptive solutions for futuristic challenges, developing high-quality semiconductor chips in India for both national and global needs.

Our mission

To build world-class, indigenous semiconductor IP from India — designing silicon that powers navigation, connectivity, and intelligent systems for a global market, while strengthening India's chip design ecosystem.

Capabilities

End-to-end IC design expertise

From architecture and circuit design to silicon verification — we cover the full spectrum of chip design challenges.

RF Design

High-frequency front-ends and transceivers engineered for multi-band, multi-standard performance under demanding conditions.

Analog Design

Precision analog building blocks — amplifiers, references, and data converters — optimised for power and accuracy.

Mixed-signal Design

Seamless integration of analog and digital domains for complex SoCs and integrated sensor interfaces.

Digital Design

Robust digital logic, PHYs, and control systems built for high-speed interconnect and low-latency processing.

Applications & Products

Industries we serve

Our silicon is designed to perform where it matters most — from satellites overhead to chips inside the vehicles and data centers shaping the future.

Navigation

Next-gen RF front-ends supporting multi-constellation GNSS signals from NavIC, GPS, Galileo & BeiDou.

Flagship Product

DhruvaPro

The first indigenous, fully-integrated, reconfigurable RFIC for navigation — supporting NavIC, GPS, Galileo, and BeiDou as a single universal solution.

Silicon-verified and built to production-level standards, with ESD protection, on-chip testing, and operation across extreme temperatures. Engineered using patented techniques for excellent performance at low power consumption.

  • Multi-constellation GNSS — NavIC, GPS, Galileo, BeiDou
  • Silicon-verified, production-level IC
  • On-chip ESD protection & built-in self-test
  • QFN-32 package for real-world evaluation
  • Rated for operation at extreme temperatures

Technology & Design Process

How we take an idea to silicon

Every design we deliver moves through a disciplined flow built to catch issues early and deliver production-ready silicon.

01

Architecture & Specification

We define system requirements, target performance, and power budgets in close collaboration with the application use-case — whether it's a GNSS receiver or a data-center PHY.

02

Circuit & Physical Design

Our team develops the RF, analog, mixed-signal, and digital blocks using industry-standard EDA flows, with a focus on robustness across process, voltage, and temperature corners.

03

Verification & Sign-off

Extensive simulation, on-chip self-test structures, and ESD protection are built in from the start, ensuring designs are production-ready before tape-out.

04

Silicon Validation

Fabricated dies are packaged and validated in real-world conditions — including extreme temperature testing — to confirm performance matches design intent.
