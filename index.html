About Us

MumbaiSemi is a fab-less semiconductor company designing high-performance RF, Analog, Mixed-signal, and Digital ICs for advanced communications, AI/ML hardware, autonomous vehicles, low-power IoT, and more.

Our core strength lies in developing innovative circuits to address the toughest challenges in chip design, from RF front-ends operating at extreme temperatures to high-speed PHYs built for next-generation data centers.

We research and innovate to produce disruptive solutions for futuristic challenges, developing high-quality semiconductor chips in India for both national and global needs.

Our mission

To build world-class, indigenous semiconductor IP from India, designing chips that power navigation, connectivity, and intelligent systems for a global market, while strengthening India's chip design ecosystem.

Applications & Products

Our chips are designed for where it matters most: satellites, vehicles, and data centers shaping the future.

Navigation

Multi-constellation GNSS front-ends supporting NavIC, GPS, Galileo and BeiDou.

Wireless

Advanced transceivers for 5G, Wi-Fi 7, and Bluetooth 5.x next-gen standards.

High-speed Communications

PHYs enabling chip-to-chip communication for PCIe and UCIe, cutting latency in AI/ML data centers.

Automotive

High-performance chips for ADAS cameras, sensor systems, and autonomous driving in extreme environments.

Flagship Product

DhruvaPro

The first indigenous, fully-integrated, reconfigurable RFIC for navigation, supporting NavIC, GPS, Galileo, BeiDou, and GLONASS as a single universal solution.

Chip-verified and built to production-level standards, with ESD protection, on-chip testing, and operation across extreme temperatures. Engineered using patented techniques for excellent performance at low power consumption.

  • Multi-constellation GNSS: NavIC, GPS, Galileo, BeiDou, GLONASS
  • Chip-verified, production-level IC
  • On-chip ESD protection & built-in self-test
  • QFN-32 package for real-world evaluation
  • Rated for operation at extreme temperatures

Open Position Full-time, In-person, Mumbai

Analog Design Engineer

MumbaiSemi, SINE — IIT Bombay Campus, Powai, Mumbai

MumbaiSemi is a next-generation semiconductor chip design start-up spun out of the aiCAS Lab, IIT Bombay. We are hiring motivated individuals to join our team. This is a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience with cutting-edge chip design technologies as a full-time member of our engineering team.

Role Description

  • Design comprehensive and precise analog circuits with clear documentation.
  • Collaborate with the team to develop design specifications that fulfil customer requirements and competitive performance targets.
  • Characterize chip prototypes and develop test specifications in coordination with the team.
  • Contribute to chip debugging and failure analysis to troubleshoot issues and enhance overall product reliability.

Essential Skills

  • Motivated, punctual, responsible, and a team player.
  • Strong communication and writing skills.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Commitment to meeting design schedules.

Technical Skills

  • Strong fundamentals for Analog Design: Deep understanding of basic analog circuits, CMOS/MOSFET behavior, and PVT variation analysis, Op-Amps, LDOs, BGRs, current mirrors, frequency synthesizers, and ADC/DAC basics.
  • Proficiency with EDA tools and simulations: Cadence Virtuoso, Calibre, etc.
  • Hands-on experience in Analog layout techniques: Transistor matching, parasitic extraction, and good layout practices.
  • Basic scripting skills for simulation automation: Python/PERL/SKILL, etc.

Eligibility

  • At least 2 years of professional experience in Analog IC design with B.Tech/B.E.
  • M.Tech/M.E in a related discipline with an excellent record.
  • Hands-on experience with circuit design, layout, tape-out, and post-chip testing will be an added advantage.
