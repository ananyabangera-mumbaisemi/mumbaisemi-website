Analog Design Engineer

MumbaiSemi, SINE — IIT Bombay Campus, Powai, Mumbai

MumbaiSemi is a next-generation semiconductor chip design start-up spun out of the aiCAS Lab, IIT Bombay. We are hiring motivated individuals to join our team. This is a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience with cutting-edge chip design technologies as a full-time member of our engineering team.

Role Description

  • Design comprehensive and precise analog circuits with clear documentation.
  • Collaborate with the team to develop design specifications that fulfil customer requirements and competitive performance targets.
  • Characterize chip prototypes and develop test specifications in coordination with the team.
  • Contribute to chip debugging and failure analysis to troubleshoot issues and enhance overall product reliability.

Essential Skills

  • Motivated, punctual, responsible, and a team player.
  • Strong communication and writing skills.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Commitment to meeting design schedules.

Technical Skills

  • Strong fundamentals for Analog Design: Deep understanding of basic analog circuits, CMOS/MOSFET behavior, and PVT variation analysis, Op-Amps, LDOs, BGRs, current mirrors, frequency synthesizers, and ADC/DAC basics.
  • Proficiency with EDA tools and simulations: Cadence Virtuoso, Calibre, etc.
  • Hands-on experience in Analog layout techniques: Transistor matching, parasitic extraction, and good layout practices.
  • Basic scripting skills for simulation automation: Python/PERL/SKILL, etc.

Eligibility

  • At least 2 years of professional experience in Analog IC design with B.Tech/B.E.
  • M.Tech/M.E in a related discipline with an excellent record.
  • Hands-on experience with circuit design, layout, tape-out, and post-chip testing will be an added advantage.
